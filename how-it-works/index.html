How the vault works

One vault, two kinds of money. A small allowance you can spend now, and savings that only leave slowly, in the open, where you can stop them.

Setting up

  1. Connect your wallet

    Open Holdfast and connect Paytaca with WizardConnect. Your wallet keeps its keys; Holdfast only asks it to sign.

  2. Choose an allowance

    The most anyone can spend from your phone in a day. Pick what you'd hand over without a fight.

  3. Choose the wait

    How long bigger withdrawals take: 1 to 7 days, 3 by default. Longer is safer, shorter is handier.

  4. Write down your home code

    A second key, shown once as words to write on paper. Keep it at home, never on the phone.

  5. Invite a guardian

    Optional but recommended. Send them a link; they connect their own wallet.

  6. Set a duress PIN, then fund the vault

    Review the rules in plain words, then send BCH in. From here the contract enforces everything.

The keys, and why there are several

Phone key

Lives in your wallet. It's the key a robber gets, so the rules give it the least power: the allowance now, everything else only after the wait.

Home code

A key that never touches the phone. It can stop anything the phone starts. Holdfast asks you to check it every 30 days, so you find out it's lost before you need it.

Guardian

A person with a key that can stop and approve, but never send money anywhere on their own. More on guardians.

Panic key and duress PIN

The duress PIN opens a normal-looking vault and freezes the real one in the background. The panic key is the freeze it uses: it can freeze and nothing else.

What Holdfast never does

It never holds your keys, never runs a server that can move your money, and has no admin switch. If this website disappeared, the contract would still follow its rules, and your home code and guardian could still recover your BCH.
