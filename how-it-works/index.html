How the vault works

One vault, two kinds of money. A small allowance you can spend now, and savings that only leave slowly, in the open, where you can stop them.

Setting up

  1. Connect your wallet

    Open Holdfast and connect Paytaca with WizardConnect. Your wallet keeps its keys; Holdfast only asks it to sign.

  2. Choose an allowance

    The most anyone can spend from your phone in a day. Pick what you'd hand over without a fight.

  3. Choose the wait

    How long bigger withdrawals take: 1 to 7 days, 3 by default. Longer is safer, shorter is handier.

  4. Write down your home code

    A second key, shown once as 12 words to write on paper. Keep the paper at home. It never goes into your wallet, and you type it in only when you need it.

  5. Invite a guardian

    Optional but recommended. They open Holdfast, tap I'm someone's guardian, connect their own wallet and send you their guardian key.

  6. Set a duress PIN, then fund the vault

    Review the rules in plain words, then send BCH in. From here the contract enforces everything.

The keys, and why there are several

Phone key

Lives in your wallet. It's the key a robber gets, so the rules give it the least power: the allowance now, everything else only after the wait.

Home code

A key you keep on paper, off your wallet. It can stop any withdrawal the phone starts and freeze the vault. The demo reminds you every 30 days to check your paper; the real vault doesn't yet.

Guardian

A person with a key that can stop and approve, but never send money anywhere on their own. More on guardians.

Panic key and duress PIN

The duress PIN opens a normal-looking vault and freezes the real one in the background. The panic key is what it uses: it can freeze at duress level and pull waiting money back into the vault. It can't send anything out.

What Holdfast never does

It never keeps your keys on a server, never runs a server that can move your money, and has no admin switch.

If this website disappeared, the contract would still follow its rules. With your vault code, home code and wallet, you can run the open-source app from GitHub and still cancel, freeze and recover.
