Hand over your phone. Keep your savings.

Holdfast is a Bitcoin Cash vault. Whoever holds your phone can spend a small daily allowance. Anything more waits days in the open, and you can cancel it from home.

Three things decide who can move your money

The rules live in a Bitcoin Cash contract. No company, server or app update can bend them, including ours.

  • Your phone

    Spends the daily allowance straight away. Can start a bigger withdrawal, which then waits 1 to 7 days. You pick the wait; 3 days is the default.

  • Your home code

    A second key you keep off your phone, written down at home. It cancels withdrawals, freezes the vault and, with a guardian, recovers it.

  • A guardian

    Someone you trust. They can cancel and freeze, and approve a fast withdrawal with you in about an hour. They can never take your money.

If they make you open the app

Set a duress PIN alongside your real one. Typed under pressure, it opens a vault that looks normal and shows only the allowance. In the background it freezes everything else.

Nothing on screen tells the robber that happened. They see a small balance, take it and leave.

Where things stand

Holdfast is being built for BCH BLAZE 2026, from Nov 14 to 22. Before kickoff we only proved the risky part: that Paytaca on a phone can sign this kind of contract. All five tests passed on chipnet, the Bitcoin Cash test network.

The app's demo mode is live and follows the vault rules in your browser. The vault contract is being written now. Nothing here runs on mainnet yet.
