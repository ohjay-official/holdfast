What Holdfast is

A website and a Bitcoin Cash contract that help you set up and use a vault. We never hold your money or your keys and can't move, freeze or recover funds for you.

It's experimental

Holdfast is being built for a hackathon and hasn't been audited. It's provided as is, without any warranty, under the MIT License. Use test coins, or only amounts you can afford to lose.

Your responsibilities

  • Keep your wallet, home code and guardian details safe. If you lose enough of them, nobody can recover your funds.
  • Check every transaction your wallet asks you to sign.
  • Follow the laws where you live.

No advice

Nothing on this site is financial, legal or safety advice. In a robbery, your safety comes before your money.

Liability

To the extent the law allows, the people who build Holdfast aren't liable for any loss from using it, including bugs, lost keys, network problems or third-party wallets and relays.
