Under the hood

The rules aren't in this website or the app. They're in one CashScript contract that Bitcoin Cash miners check on every spend. Here's how it's built.

One address, four kinds of coin

Everything a vault owns sits at a single contract address. Coins there are told apart by the CashToken NFT they carry, all of one category that only the vault can mint.

  • The vault coin carries the minting NFT. Its 13-byte commitment holds the freeze level, the allowance spent in the current window, and four approval flags.
  • A waiting withdrawal carries a mutable NFT naming its one recipient. Its age is the clock.
  • A guardian-replacement ticket carries an immutable NFT. Its age is the 7-day clock.
  • A deposit carries no token. It waits until a vault key merges it in.

One address means the app reads the whole vault with a single lookup, and nobody can hide a withdrawal somewhere else.

Design choices

Approvals one signer at a time

Unfreezing needs two people, but they never have to sign the same transaction. Each approval is its own spend that flips one flag. Your guardian approves from their own phone, whenever they're free.

Time from coin age, not the clock

Waits use relative time locks on each withdrawal coin. Nothing depends on a timestamp the spender could choose.

No admin key

No function lets Holdfast, or anyone outside your keys, move or freeze your coins. If this site disappears, the contract still works with your wallet.

Fees come from the vault

Every spend caps its own fee (2,500 sats, or 4,000 for a two-coin merge), so your wallet only ever signs. It never has to add coins to pay.

Tests that try to break it

18 contract tests run every function through the Bitcoin Cash VM on CashScript's mock network, including the wrong keys, the wrong amounts and the wrong timing. Three are attacks found while reviewing the design:

  • Minting a second, fake vault coin during an allowance spend. Blocked: every spend fixes its outputs exactly.
  • Keeping a released withdrawal's NFT alive to fake a fast withdrawal. Blocked: release burns it.
  • Skipping the 7-day ticket check by spending the ticket as a merge. Blocked: a merge can only go back into the vault.
