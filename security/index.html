● Security

What Holdfast protects against

Most crypto security assumes the thief never meets you. Holdfast assumes they might: that they have your phone, your PIN and you, standing right there.

01// Scenarios

What happens when things go wrong

You lose your home codeNothingYour phone can still withdraw with the normal wait. Withdraw everything into a new vault.
A guardian turns on youNothingThey can freeze or cancel, but not send. Remove them with phone and home code.
This website goes awayNothingThe contract is on Bitcoin Cash. Your wallet and keys still work with it.
02// Limits

What it can't stop

Holdfast limits what a robber gains. It doesn't make you safe from them, and it can't help if they hold you for longer than your wait or find your home code too.

  • Keep the home code somewhere a robber wouldn't reach in the same trip.
  • Pick a guardian who won't be with you when it happens.
  • If you're in danger, hand over the allowance. That's what it's for.

Money already sent out of the vault, including the allowance, is gone. Bitcoin Cash payments can't be reversed.

Still to do

The vault contract is written, with tests that include attempts to break it, but it hasn't had an outside audit. Until it has, treat Holdfast as an experiment and keep only small amounts in it.
