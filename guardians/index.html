  • ✓ A guardian can

    • Cancel a withdrawal while it waits
    • Freeze the vault
    • Approve a fast withdrawal with you, in about an hour
    • Unfreeze or recover the vault with your home code

  • ✕ A guardian can't

    • Send money anywhere, alone or with anyone but you
    • Change your allowance or wait
    • See anything that isn't already public on Bitcoin Cash
    • Stop you from removing them, given 7 days

Choosing one

Pick someone who answers their phone, who won't usually be with you, and who you'd trust with a spare house key. A sibling in another city is a good guardian. Your flatmate is a weaker one.

You don't have to have a guardian. Without one, your home code does their job alone, and fast withdrawals aren't available.

If you've been asked to be one

  1. Open the invite link

    It comes from the vault owner. It only shows a request; nothing happens until you accept.

  2. Connect your wallet

    Paytaca, with WizardConnect. Your wallet signs to show which key is yours. You don't send any money.

  3. Turn on alerts

    So you hear when a withdrawal starts. If the owner calls and says it isn't them, cancel it.

Status

Guardians work in the demo and in the real vault app. The vault tested on mainnet so far has no guardian; a mainnet guardian test is next. Two-signer spends, which guardian approval relies on, passed on chipnet before kickoff. See the transactions.
